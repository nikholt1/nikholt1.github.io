Overview

01 / ABOUT

During the second semester of my Datamatiker education at EK, we developed three full-stack applications. ProjectWizard was our final examination project: a project management web application developed for Alpha Solutions, a digital consultancy working with business solutions and large-scale partners.

Working in a team of four, we were given just three weeks to deliver a functional application. We used SCRUM and an agile development process to organize our work, prioritize requirements, and deliver a minimum viable product (MVP) within the deadline.

Alongside the application, we produced technical and methodological documentation covering feasibility, stakeholder interests, risk analysis, user stories, system design, database design, UI/UX, usability, testing, and the SCRUM process.

Development Process

02 / SCRUM
01
ANALYSIS

Feasibility and Risk Analysis

We began with a feasibility study, stakeholder interest study, and risk analysis to establish the project's scope, constraints, and priorities before development began.

02
REQUIREMENTS

User Stories and Product Backlog

Based on Alpha Solutions' requirements, we formulated user stories and created a product backlog. We estimated task complexity and effort using Planning Poker.

03
SPRINT PLANNING

Prioritization and Sprint Planning

During our SCRUM meetings, we prioritized backlog items according to the MVP requirements. This helped us focus our limited development time on the most important functionality.

04
DEVELOPMENT

Full-Stack Implementation

Over approximately two and a half weeks, we implemented the application, integrating the frontend, backend, database, and supporting functionality while working through our backlog.

05
VALIDATION

MVP and Acceptance Testing

We reached our goal of delivering a functional full-stack web application. The MVP went through acceptance testing, and we completed the outstanding tasks in the backlog.

06
EXAMINATION

Report and Final Presentation

After development, we completed our examination report and prepared a 30-minute presentation covering the application, a demonstration, errors encountered and resolved, and our conclusions. We were then individually examined on our programming methods and technical choices.

Testing and Quality Assurance

04 / QA

Testing was an important part of validating the application and ensuring that its functionality met the project requirements.

  • Unit tests: Testing individual components and units of application logic.
  • Integration tests: Verifying that application components work together correctly.
  • Web-layer tests: Testing web-layer behaviour and interactions with the application.
  • Acceptance tests: Validating the MVP against the requirements and expected functionality.

System Design and Documentation

05 / ENGINEERING

The project included both implementation and the analysis required to justify and document our design decisions. Our report covered the following areas:

  • Feasibility, stakeholder interest, and risk studies.
  • User stories and product backlog management.
  • SCRUM planning and sprint documentation.
  • Entity-relationship diagram and domain model.
  • Database design and implementation.
  • UI/UX design and usability study.
  • Heuristic evaluation and acceptance testing.

The examination report documented our process, technical decisions, system design, and testing approach across the development lifecycle.
